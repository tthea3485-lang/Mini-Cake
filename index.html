About Our Bakery🥯

Discover the story behind Matty's delicious creations

Baking with Passion Since 2020

At Mini Cake, we believe that every celebration deserves a delicious centerpiece. Founded in 2020, our bakery has been dedicated to creating exceptional cakes and pastries that bring joy to our customers in Phnom Penh.

Our team of skilled bakers uses only the finest ingredients, sourced locally whenever possible, to ensure every bite is as delightful as the last.

We're committed to exceptional customer service, working closely with you to create custom cakes for weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, and all of life's special moments.
